# Venture B — Digital Products

**Model:** make-once, sell-infinitely downloadable assets (templates, spreadsheets, planners,
kits) sold self-serve via Lemon Squeezy.
**Who pays:** buyers searching to buy/download a specific asset.
**Why passive:** zero marginal cost, instant delivery, near-zero support.
