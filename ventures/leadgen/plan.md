# Venture A — Programmatic-SEO Lead-Gen

**Model:** rank many static, genuinely useful pages for high-intent service queries →
capture leads → route/sell them to providers who pay per lead.
**Who pays:** the service provider with acute, billable pain (a lead is worth $20–150+).
**Why passive:** pages are permanent compounding assets; no per-customer support.
