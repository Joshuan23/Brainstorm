# Opportunity Backlog (ranked, evidence-backed)

Maintained by `niche-scout`. Builders pull their next target from the top of each list.
Every entry needs: target query, who currently ranks (weak/strong), evidence of
willingness-to-pay, the specific wedge, and a verdict.

## Venture A — Lead-gen wedges
1. **[VALIDATED · TOP] Water damage / restoration — hyperlocal + cause-specific.**
   Leads $400–$900+ each (avg ~$542); pay-per-call converts 5–7×. Head terms owned by big
   players → target small-city + cause long-tail ("burst pipe cleanup [small city]",
   "basement flood restoration [small city]", "sewage backup cleanup"). Template:
   `app/restoration/[cause]/[city]`. Needs: lead buyer/partner + TCPA consent (owner).
   Evidence: `2026-07-08-leadgen-restoration.md`.

2. **[VALIDATED] Mold remediation — hyperlocal + symptom/cause-specific, extends the
   restoration template.** Leads $30–$350 (form $50–200, calls $100–350; avg ~$45.51 for
   standard leads). Small-city SERPs for symptom/cause long-tail ("mold after water leak",
   "black mold basement [small city]", "attic/crawl-space mold") are dominated by directories
   (Angi/Yelp/Thumbtack) and generic cost guides, not dense franchise networks — thin/weak.
   Wedge: add mold causes to the existing `app/restoration/[cause]/[city]` template and
   cross-link from water-damage pages (mold is the natural downstream problem after a leak).
   Same lead-buyer ecosystem as restoration (Service Direct, 99calls, eForce, Inquirly).
   Evidence: `2026-07-08-next-leadgen-vertical.md`.

Backlog to validate next: junk removal, tree service (lower value, lower competition —
confirmed: tree $18–100/lead, junk $10–70/lead, garage door $10–70/lead). Roofing storm
damage is high-value ($90–300/lead) but small-city SERPs already owned by storm-restoration
franchise networks (Storm Guard, SERVPRO) — revisit opportunistically after a named storm
event in an underserved metro, not as a default build. Foundation/basement waterproofing
rejected below.

## Venture B — Digital-product wedges
1. **[VALIDATED · TOP] Focused "Pricing & Profit Calculator + Client Tracker"** (Sheets +
   Notion), one solopreneur niche at a time, $19–29 via Lemon Squeezy. Category proven
   (finance + productivity + lightweight CRM = ~⅔ of sales); focused > all-in-one. Avoid
   generic budgeting (free-saturated). Clone per niche = new page + listing.
   Evidence: `2026-07-08-products-templates.md`.

2. **[VALIDATED] Bookkeeper Pricing & Package Calculator** — second niche clone of the
   flagship pricing/profit calculator, same Sheets+Notion mechanic, $19–29 (possibly $29–39
   given CPA-branded comps). Real paid Etsy competitors exist (interactive calculators, not
   just guides), built by credentialed sellers (e.g. a CPA-branded listing) — confirms
   willingness to pay. Crucially, unlike the cleaning-business variant, no dominant free-SaaS
   calculator crowds this niche (only a blog-post guide from Financial Cents), so less
   crowding-out risk. Wedge: swap fields to bookkeeping overhead (software subscriptions,
   E&O insurance, package tiers, add-ons like payroll/cleanup) and target "bookkeeper pricing
   calculator" / "how much to charge for bookkeeping services". Evidence:
   `2026-07-08-next-product-niche.md`.

Backlog to validate next: content calendar for a niche (independently proven category —
new sellers with a single channel-specific calendar: $250–900 in first 3 months, mid-tier
$1,800–4,500/mo — but different mechanic/audience than the calculator, build after the
bookkeeping variant ships); photographer pricing/profit calculator (high search volume but
most incumbents are static price-list designs, not real calculators — real wedge exists but
weaker validation, worth a future variant); single-purpose second-brain variant.

## Rejected (red oceans — do not build)
- Bank/brokerage/crypto **statement → CSV converters**: 15+ players, many free,
  commoditized by LLMs, price crushed to $9–29/mo. Moat (parsing templates) erased by
  template-free LLM parsing.
- Generic Etsy/Amazon **fee calculators**: a dozen free tools + an official built-in one.
- KDP cover/spine tools: multiple free incumbents.
- Generic contractor estimate/quote generators: crowded (ArcSite, Buildxact, Jotform, etc.).
- Foundation/basement waterproofing lead-gen at small-city long-tail: already densely covered
  by well-funded franchise networks (Groundworks, Thrasher, Helitech, Bay Crawl Space,
  Southeast Foundation & Crawl Space Repair) with existing pages even in towns of ~5,000
  people — the exact whitespace we rely on elsewhere doesn't exist here.
- Cleaning-business pricing calculator (as a standalone product): paid demand exists but
  crowded by dominant free calculators from well-funded SaaS incumbents (ZenMaid, Jobber,
  Housecall Pro, InvoiceMama) that outrank paid alternatives on head terms.
- Freelance proposal/estimate templates: 2,000+ Etsy listings, top Gumroad result for the
  category is free ("$20K+ Freelance Proposal Template (FREE)") — same red-ocean pattern as
  contractor estimate generators.
