# Research — Venture A: second lead-gen vertical (2026-07-08)

**Verdict: PURSUE mold remediation (hyperlocal + symptom/cause long-tail). Foundation/basement
waterproofing and roofing storm damage = deprioritize/skip at this stage (see below).**

## Method
Searched head terms + long-tail for 5 candidates: mold remediation, foundation/basement
waterproofing, roofing storm damage, tree removal, junk removal, garage door repair (last
three mainly as low-value confirmation/comparison against the existing backlog note).

## Candidate 1 — Mold remediation (RECOMMENDED)
- **Lead price:** $30–$100 for a standard qualified lead; form leads $50–$200; phone/call
  leads $100–$350. One provider average ~$45.51/lead. Budgets of $1,500–$10,000/mo reported
  by agencies selling into this space. [Service Direct](https://blog.servicedirect.com/how-much-do-service-direct-mold-removal-leads-cost),
  [BuiltRight Digital](https://builtrightdigital.com/mold-remediation-lead-generation-cost/),
  [33 Mile Radius](https://www.33mileradius.com/mold-leads/cost-of-mold-removal-leads/),
  [99calls ($129.99 exclusive)](https://99calls.com/Mold-Remediation-Leads.htm),
  [eForce](https://www.eforceweb.com/mold-remediation-leads).
- **Who ranks on small-city/symptom long-tail:** For queries like "mold testing after water
  damage," "black mold in basement removal cost [small town]," results are dominated by
  **directories (Angi, Yelp, Thumbtack) and generic cost-guide content** (HomeGuide, This Old
  House, Bob Vila, HomeAdvisor) rather than dense franchise city-page networks. A few
  regional players (Green Home Solutions, SERVPRO) have city pages but coverage is thinner
  and less exhaustive than the foundation-repair franchises below. **Weak/thin SERP = opening.**
- **The wedge:** Mold is the natural downstream problem after water damage — "found mold
  after a leak," "mold after flood," "attic mold," "bathroom exhaust mold," "crawl space
  mold" × small city. This lets us **cross-link directly from the existing
  `app/restoration/[cause]/[city]` hub** (a restoration/water-damage searcher is a near-perfect
  mold-remediation lead too), reusing the same template shape (symptom/cause × city) and much
  of the existing content/FAQ scaffolding.
- **Buyer confirmed:** Yes — same lead-buyer ecosystem as restoration (Service Direct,
  99calls, eForce, Inquirly, Goodzer, Payperlead all sell mold leads), so no new
  partner-sourcing problem.

## Candidate 2 — Foundation / basement waterproofing (SKIP for now)
- **Lead price:** $23–$45 typical shared lead; $25–$150 at Service Direct; $41–$99 at
  Minyona. Mid-value, below mold and well below restoration.
  [ProMatcher](https://www.promatcher.com/marketing/waterproofing/how-much-do-basement-waterproofing-leads-cost-3373),
  [Service Direct](https://servicedirect.com/basement-waterproofing-leads/),
  [Minyona](https://minyona.com/waterproofing-leads).
- **Who ranks on small-city long-tail:** This is the key finding — searching "crawl space
  foundation crack repair [small city]" surfaced **national franchise networks with dense,
  already-built small-city pages**: Groundworks (Sioux City IA), Thrasher (Waynesville MO,
  pop. ~5,000), Helitech (Kansas City metro suburbs), Bay Crawl Space (Chesapeake VA),
  Southeast Foundation & Crawl Space Repair (Fayetteville/Wilmington/Greenville NC). These
  are well-funded franchise/rollup networks that have *already* executed the exact
  service-area-page playbook we'd use, down to small towns. **This is the opposite of
  whitespace — it's a red ocean at the precise long-tail level we'd target.**
- **Verdict:** Skip. The wedge we rely on elsewhere (small city + specific cause, thin SERP)
  does not exist here; it's already been farmed by incumbents with more resources.

## Candidate 3 — Roofing storm damage (SKIP / lower priority)
- **Lead price:** Highest of the group — $90–$200 for an exclusive storm/replacement lead,
  spiking 30–60% within 48–72 hrs of a hail/wind event; insurance-claim leads $100–$300.
  [StormLead](https://stormlead.co/), [getbiddable.com](https://getbiddable.com/blog-post.php?slug=roofing-lead-cost-benchmarks-2026),
  [Minyona](https://minyona.com/roofing-leads).
- **Who ranks:** "Emergency roof tarp storm damage repair near me [small city]" is already
  dominated by **dedicated storm-restoration franchise networks** (Storm Guard, SERVPRO,
  regional roofing outfits) that run the same small-city storm-response page playbook, plus
  roofing overall is one of the most heavily-contested PPC/SEO verticals nationally (huge
  agency spend, thousands of roofing-SEO shops).
- **Verdict:** Highest lead value of the set, but SEO wedge is weak — competitors already
  cover small cities, and demand is event-driven/spiky (dependent on storm events hitting
  specific regions), making a programmatic evergreen page strategy less reliable than mold.
  Worth revisiting opportunistically (e.g., building storm-specific pages fast after a named
  hail/wind event in an underserved metro) but not as the next default build.

## Comparison set (lower value, confirms backlog note)
- **Tree removal:** $18–$100/lead (avg ~$58 nationally); residential $18-29, exclusive up to
  $75. [HomeServiceDirect](https://www.homeservicedirect.net/how-much-are-tree-service-leads-worth/).
- **Junk removal:** $10–$70/lead, national avg ~$33.75.
  [PushLeads](https://pushleads.com/junk-removal-marketing/junk-removal-cost-per-lead/).
- **Garage door repair:** $10–$70/lead ($20–70 typical exclusive).
  [ProMatcher](https://www.promatcher.com/marketing/garage-doors/how-much-do-garage-door-leads-cost-3025).
- All three confirmed lower-value than mold/foundation/roofing — deprioritize as expected.

## Recommendation
Build **mold remediation** as the second lead-gen vertical, extending the existing
restoration template rather than starting a fresh silo:
`app/restoration/[cause]/[city]` gains mold-specific causes ("mold after water leak," "attic
mold," "basement mold," "crawl space mold," "bathroom mold") alongside the existing
water-damage causes, targeting small cities where directories/cost-guides rank instead of
franchise networks. Same monetization path as restoration (Service Direct, 99calls, eForce,
Inquirly all buy mold leads) — same owner decision needed on lead-buyer partner + TCPA
consent already flagged in the restoration research.
