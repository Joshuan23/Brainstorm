# Research — Venture A wedge: water damage / restoration lead-gen (2026-07-08)

**Verdict: PURSUE (hyperlocal + cause-specific long-tail).**

## Why
- **Lead value is exceptional.** Exclusive water-damage leads: ~$400–$900+ each (national
  avg ~$542; competitive metros like Dallas ~$775). 3–10× roofing/HVAC ($30–300).
- **Urgent intent converts.** Pay-per-call converts 5–7× better than form leads; 25–50% of
  calls book. Emergency searchers act now.
- **Natural programmatic template.** service × cause × city scales to thousands of pages.

## Competition & the wedge
- Head terms owned by aggressive lead-gen players (33 Mile Radius, ServiceDirect,
  ResultCalls, 99calls, eForce, Inquirly). Do **not** fight "water damage leads [big city]".
- **Whitespace = small-city + cause-specific long-tail:** "burst pipe cleanup [small city]",
  "basement flood restoration [small city]", "sewage backup cleanup", "storm/flood damage
  restoration [small city]". Thin SERPs, high intent, still $400+ leads.

## Monetization path
- Route as **pay-per-call / form** to a restoration lead buyer or local partner. Options:
  resell via an existing marketplace (ServiceDirect/33 Mile Radius) or a direct local
  contractor relationship. **Owner decision required** before we can bank revenue.

## Risks / owner escalations
- **TCPA / consent** on collecting phone numbers — get the consent language right per state.
- Need a real lead buyer or partner to monetize (pages can rank first, monetize second).

## Recommended first build (seo-page-builder)
`app/restoration/[cause]/[city]` static template: what-to-do-now checklist + causes + FAQ +
`Service`/`FAQPage` JSON-LD + a low-friction "get emergency help" call/form CTA. Seed with
one cause ("burst pipe cleanup") across ~20 small cities; expand causes/cities daily.
