# Research — Venture B wedge: focused single-purpose systems (2026-07-08)

**Verdict: PURSUE (focused single-purpose, one niche at a time).**

## Why
- Across Gumroad/Etsy/Notion, ~⅔ of paid sales are **productivity, personal finance,
  content calendars, lightweight CRMs**. Buyers pay $10–50 for a well-built Notion template;
  Google Sheets templates sell well too.
- **Focused single-purpose systems outperform all-in-one "life OS"** templates.
- Realistic outcome shipping ~1/quarter with modest marketing: $4k–18k/12mo — and we ship
  far faster than quarterly.

## The wedge
- Avoid **generic budgeting/bookkeeping** — free templates saturate it.
- Target a **specific solopreneur niche's recurring money task**. The clearest cited pain:
  "setting rates that are both competitive and profitable is tricky." → a **pricing/profit
  calculator + client tracker** system, sold per niche.
- Cross-venture synergy: a service-business flavor cross-links with Venture A's hubs.

## Recommended first product (product-maker)
A focused **"Pricing & Profit Calculator + Client Tracker"** (Google Sheets + Notion versions)
for **one** starter niche — recommend **freelance/creative service providers** (designers,
photographers, VAs) or **home-service solopreneurs** (ties to Venture A). Price $19–29 via
Lemon Squeezy, instant download. Ship one niche variant, then clone to adjacent niches
(each variant = a new SEO page + listing).

## Delivery / monetization
- Lemon Squeezy (already wired) as merchant of record; instant download on purchase.
- One product page per niche (marketplace-growth) targeting "[niche] pricing calculator /
  client tracker template".

## Owner escalations
- Confirm starter niche + price band. Whether to also list on Gumroad/Etsy or stay on-site.
