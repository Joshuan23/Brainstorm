# Research — Venture B: second digital-product niche (2026-07-08)

**Verdict: PURSUE a "Bookkeeper Pricing & Package Calculator" niche clone. Content calendar
= strong #2 (different mechanic, proven category, good follow-on). Cleaning-business pricing
calculator = deprioritize (crowded by dominant free SaaS tools).**

## Method
Evaluated three candidates against the existing product's proven mechanic ("setting
profitable rates is hard" pain + Sheets/Notion calculator, $19–29): (1) pricing calculators
cloned into adjacent solo-service niches — photographers, cleaners, bookkeepers; (2) a
focused content calendar for a niche; (3) a freelance proposal/estimate template.

## Candidate 1 — Niche-cloned pricing calculators
### Bookkeepers (RECOMMENDED)
- **Paid demand confirmed:** Multiple distinct paid Etsy listings, not just guides —
  "Bookkeeper Pricing Calculator Monthly Service Rate" (Excel/Sheets), "Pearly Gates Boutique
  Bookkeeper Pricing Calculator" (input hourly rate/services/fees → auto-quote), built by a
  seller marketed as a Chartered Professional Accountant (15+ yrs). This is a real
  interactive calculator, not a decorative price sheet, and multiple sellers compete for it.
  [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1864471641/bookkeeper-pricing-calculator-monthly).
- **Free-competitor check:** The main free competitor found is a **blog-post guide**
  (Financial Cents' "Free Bookkeeping Pricing Template Guide") — a content asset, not a
  polished interactive tool. No dominant free-SaaS calculator crowding this niche the way
  ZenMaid/Jobber do for cleaning (see below). **Lower crowding-out risk than cleaning.**
- **Recurring demand signal:** "How much to charge for bookkeeping services" / package-tier
  pricing (basic/plus/premium + add-ons like payroll, cleanup projects, CPE/software
  overhead) is a persistent pain for the fast-growing solo/virtual-bookkeeper segment.
- **Wedge:** Reuse the exact Sheets+Notion mechanic from the flagship calculator, swap fields
  to bookkeeping-specific overhead (software subscriptions, E&O insurance, client-tier
  packages) and comparable-rate benchmarks. Fastest possible build — no new mechanic, just a
  new niche variant + landing page, exactly per the existing "clone per niche" growth plan.

### Photographers (weaker pick, noted for backlog)
- **Very high listing volume** on Etsy (separate market pages for photography_pricing_template,
  _calculator, _guide, _price_list — signals strong recurring search volume) but the large
  majority are **static Canva/PDF price *sheets*, not profit/overhead calculators** — i.e.
  design-forward price lists to hand a client, not a "what should I actually charge to be
  profitable" tool. [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/photography_pricing_calculator).
- Real whitespace exists (a true margin/overhead calculator vs. the sea of price-list
  designs) but validation is weaker/more inferred than bookkeeping's direct calculator
  comps. Worth a future variant, not the top pick today.

### Cleaning businesses (deprioritize)
- **Paid demand exists** — a specific Etsy listing ("Cleaning Business Pricing Calculator
  Excel Quote and Cost Template," automated labor/supply/tax calc, listed Feb 2026) confirms
  people do pay. [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4388244254/cleaning-business-pricing-calculator).
- **But the category is crowded by dominant FREE tools from well-funded SaaS incumbents**:
  ZenMaid ("Free Cleaning Business Pricing Calculator"), InvoiceMama ("House Cleaning Cost
  Calculator — Free"), Jobber and Housecall Pro both publish detailed free pricing guides as
  lead magnets for their paid field-service software. These free tools rank prominently for
  the exact head terms a paid product would target. This is the same pattern flagged in the
  Rejected list for generic contractor estimate/quote generators. **Willingness-to-pay is
  real but weaker than bookkeeping because free substitutes are more numerous and more
  polished.**

## Candidate 2 — Content calendar for a niche
- **Category is strongly proven independent of this research** (confirmed via search):
  content calendars are one of the ~4 categories driving roughly two-thirds of paid template
  sales across Gumroad/Etsy/Notion marketplace. New sellers with a single channel-specific
  calendar: $250–900 in first 3 months; mid-tier: $1,800–4,500/mo; top sellers: $6,000–
  15,000/mo. Typical price $10–25 (multi-platform planners up to $25).
  [SendOwl](https://www.sendowl.com/blog/tips-and-advice/best-selling-notion-template-categories).
- **Buyer profile:** creators/social-media managers/small agencies — a different audience
  than the solo-service-provider buyer of the pricing calculator, so less cross-sell synergy
  with the existing product/audience, but a legitimately strong standalone category.
- **Verdict:** Good validated #2 option — recommend as the *next* build after the bookkeeping
  calculator ships, not instead of it, since it requires a new mechanic/design rather than
  reusing the proven calculator template.

## Candidate 3 — Freelance proposal/estimate template (SKIP)
- **Saturation:** Etsy alone lists "2,000+" freelance proposal template items ("5,000+"
  freelance templates broadly). [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/freelance_proposal_template).
- **Weak willingness-to-pay signal:** The top-surfacing Gumroad result for this exact query
  is a **free** template ("The $20K+ Freelance Proposal Template (FREE)"), and generic
  proposal/estimate tooling is already flagged in the Rejected list (contractor estimate
  generators — ArcSite, Buildxact, Jotform, etc.) as commoditized.
- **Verdict:** Skip — matches the existing red-ocean pattern (many free options, thin
  differentiation, crowded market).

## Recommendation
Ship the **Bookkeeper Pricing & Package Calculator** (Sheets + Notion) as the second niche
variant of the proven system — same $19–29 price band, same Lemon Squeezy delivery, new
landing page targeting "bookkeeper pricing calculator" / "how much to charge for bookkeeping
services" / "virtual bookkeeper rate calculator." Queue **a content calendar** (new niche
TBD by owner/product-maker) as the validated #3 build once the second calculator variant
ships — different mechanic but independently proven category.
