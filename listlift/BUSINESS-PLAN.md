# ListLift — Business Plan

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**One line:** A $19/mo AI listing optimizer for Etsy/Shopify/Amazon sellers, acquired
through free programmatic-SEO tool pages that rank on high-intent queries.

**This is a *new* direction** — deliberately not another forex/trading product (PipSignal,
the prop-trader analytics plan). It reuses the same license-key paywall pattern (now on
Gumroad, the easiest merchant of record) but attacks a large, proven, non-regulated market.

## Who owns what ("run by me")

The honest split that makes this real:

| You (the human) — one-time, ~1 hour | Me (the AI) — ongoing operation |
|---|---|
| Register the Gumroad account (merchant of record) | Build & maintain the product and engine |
| Point a domain at Vercel, set 4 env vars | Generate new SEO tool pages (more funnels) on a schedule |
| Approve the first community launch posts | Draft launch/outreach copy, changelog, support replies |
| Be the legal entity / receive payouts | Monitor trial→paid, iterate pricing & copy |

I can't hold a bank account or be the merchant of record — that shell is yours. Everything
that *runs* the business (product, content, acquisition assets, iteration) I do.

## Why this market

- **They already pay.** eRank ($5.99–$9.99/mo), Marmalead ($19/mo), Alura ($13.99+/mo) —
  Etsy-SEO tools are an established, profitable category. We enter at a clean $19 with a
  faster "idea → full listing" workflow and multi-marketplace support.
- **Huge, motivated audience.** ~9M+ active Etsy sellers, millions more on Shopify/Amazon,
  most fighting the same problem: their listings don't get found.
- **The pain is on-page and fixable.** Titles that bury the keyword, empty tag slots, thin
  descriptions. That's exactly what a rules-based engine fixes — no "advice", no regulation.
- **Impulse price point.** A seller spending on ads/inventory approves $19/mo without a meeting.

## The product

1. **Free single-listing optimizer** (acquisition + virality) — unlimited, no account. This
   is what the SEO pages expose, so they're genuinely useful and link-worthy.
2. **Pro bulk optimizer** ($19/mo) — paste a whole shop (up to 200 rows) or CSV, export
   optimized titles/tags/descriptions as a spreadsheet. The time-saver power sellers pay for.
3. **Transparent SEO score** — the trust differentiator. We show which rules each listing
   passes instead of promising rankings.

## The autopilot loop

```
Google ("etsy title generator", "etsy tag generator for jewelry",
        "shopify product description generator")
  → free tool page (static, costs nothing to serve)
    → CTA ("optimize your whole shop") → $19/mo Pro
      → retained by bulk workflow + CSV export + saved history (roadmap)
```

Delivery is automated (pure software). Acquisition is automated (SEO pages rank 24/7, and I
can generate more niche pages on demand). The only non-automatable phase is the **first
~50 customers** — manual reps in Etsy-seller communities (Reddit r/Etsy, r/EtsySellers, FB
groups, TikTok). Skipping that is why most SaaS dies; budget 4–6 weeks of it, then the loop carries.

## Pricing

$19/mo, one tier, one CTA. Free tier stays unlimited for single listings (it's the funnel,
not a crippled trial). Revisit an annual plan and a $39 "agency/bulk-heavy" tier only after
100 subscribers.

## Milestones (targets, not guarantees)

| When | Milestone | MRR |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Deployed, Gumroad live, sitemap submitted | $0 |
| Week 4 | First 25 paying (manual community outreach) | ~$475 |
| Month 3 | 120 subscribers, 30+ SEO pages indexed | ~$2,280 |
| Month 6 | 350 subscribers, SEO compounding | ~$6,650 |
| Month 12 | 1,000 subscribers → **~$228k ARR, a few hrs/week** | ~$19,000 |

The model works only if trial/free→paid holds above ~3–5% of tool-page traffic and pages
actually index. Both are checked monthly (see `LAUNCH.md`).

## Risks & honest mitigations

- **SEO takes months to compound.** Mitigation: manual community launch drives the first
  cohort while pages age in.
- **Marketplace dependency (Etsy could change rules).** Mitigation: multi-marketplace from
  day one; the engine is rules-data we control.
- **"Just use ChatGPT."** Mitigation: bulk workflow, per-marketplace constraints, CSV export,
  and the transparent score — none of which a raw chatbot gives a busy seller.
- **Commoditization.** Mitigation: compete on workflow + trust (the score), not on the model.

## Startup cost

Effectively $0 to launch: Vercel free/hobby tier, Gumroad takes a cut only on sales,
domain ~$12/yr. The business funds itself from the first subscriber.
